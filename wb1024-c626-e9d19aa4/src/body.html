🚀 Ten Satellites, One Ride

素材来源：央广网 2026-10-15、央视新闻 2026-10-14、中国新闻网 2026-10-14（真实新闻改写，难度适配初三）
On the evening of October 14, at 19:40, a Chinese commercial rocket lit up the sky. The Zhuque-2E Y4, an improved carrier rocket, lifted off from the Dongfeng Commercial Space Innovation Test Zone and sent ten satellites into their planned orbits. The flight test was a complete success.
The rocket was built by LandSpace, a private Chinese company. This was the fourth flight of the improved Zhuque-2E model, and the tenth flight of the whole Zhuque-2 family. Only twenty-nine days earlier, an earlier version of the same rocket had flown — a sign of how quickly China's commercial launches are now repeating.
The ten satellites did not belong to one owner. They were "ride-sharing" passengers from different customers. Eight of them were made by ADASpace for its "Star Computing" plan, meant to build a space-based computing network. Two more, named Guangchuan-03 and -04, were laser-communication test satellites built by another private firm.
The Zhuque-2E is a two-stage, low-temperature liquid rocket. It burns liquid oxygen and methane, and can carry up to 6 tonnes to low Earth orbit, or 4 tonnes to a 500-kilometer sun-synchronous orbit. With this power, it has become a main workhorse among China's medium commercial rockets.
Ride-sharing launches are becoming normal. Different satellites vary in weight, shape and release needs, and fitting them all into one fairing tests a company's coordination. As space-based computing and satellite internet grow, more small satellites will share one ride — cheaper and faster than flying alone.

一、阅读理解 7 题 · 14 分

1细节理解
When did the Zhuque-2E Y4 rocket lift off?
【定位】第1段：'On the evening of October 14, at 19:40, a Chinese commercial rocket lit up the sky.'
【判据】题干问发射时刻，原句直接给出 10月14日 19:40。
【排错】B 项10月15日是媒体报道日；C 项9月14日原文未提；D 项把时刻当成每天。
【举一反三】考点：时间细节。一段里出现多个日期时，先圈题干问的是发射日还是报道日。
2数字细节
How many days between this flight and the previous Zhuque-2E flight?
【定位】第2段：'Only twenty-nine days earlier, an earlier version of the same rocket had flown.'
【判据】题干问两次发射间隔，原句是 twenty-nine days。
【排错】A 项10是卫星总数与火箭型号尾号；C 项4是型号 Y4；D 项6是近地轨道运力（吨）。
【举一反三】考点：数字细节。题干里出现 between / gap 时，答案常是间隔天数，别拿总数或型号号充数。
3词义猜测
What does the word "ride-sharing" mean in Paragraph 3?
【定位】第3段：'They were "ride-sharing" passengers from different customers.'
【判据】ride 指火箭这一“趟”，sharing 指多家客户共用，合起来即多颗卫星拼一枚火箭。
【排错】A 项卖票、B 项造火箭、D 项去同一城市，均偏离“共享搭载”的本义。
【举一反三】考点：词义猜测。先拆词（ride 一趟 / share 共享），再放回“不同客户、一次发射”的语境检验。
4推理判断
Why does the writer mention "liquid oxygen and methane"?
【定位】第4段：'It burns liquid oxygen and methane, and can carry up to 6 tonnes...'
【判据】作者列举推进剂，是为了说明这枚火箭“烧什么”，即燃料类型。
【排错】A 项便宜、B 项与外国对比、C 项发射时间，原文都未提。
【举一反三】考点：推理判断。问 why-mention 而原文无 because 时，看该词所处句子在介绍火箭的哪方面属性。
5细节理解
How heavy a load can the rocket carry to low Earth orbit?
【定位】第4段：'can carry up to 6 tonnes to low Earth orbit, or 4 tonnes to a 500-kilometer sun-synchronous orbit.'
【判据】题干问近地轨道运力，原句给出 6 tonnes。
【排错】B 项4吨是500公里太阳同步轨道；C 项10是卫星数；D 项29是两次发射间隔天数。
【举一反三】考点：数字细节。轨道名称（low Earth / sun-synchronous）是定位关键词，先锁定再取数。
6态度与意图
What is the writer's attitude toward ride-sharing launches?
【定位】第5段：'Ride-sharing launches are becoming normal... cheaper and faster than flying alone.'
【判据】作者用 becoming normal、cheaper、faster 等词，态度是肯定、支持的。
【排错】A 项担忧、C 项无所谓、D 项批评，都与“更便宜更快”的基调相反。
【举一反三】考点：态度意图。抓评价性形容词/比较级（cheaper/faster），正负态度一眼可辨。
7预测出处
Where would this passage most likely appear?
【定位】全文：报道一次真实的火箭发射，含时间、主体、数据、意义。
【判据】纪实+数据+时效，是典型科技新闻报道的特征。
【排错】A 项小说、B 项菜谱、D 项旅游指南，文体与内容均不符。
【举一反三】考点：预测出处。看文体信号：人名机构名+具体日期+数据，多半是新闻报道。

三、语法词汇 5 题 · 10 分

1时态
The rocket ___ ten satellites into their planned orbits.
【还原】全文叙述过去发射，谓语用一般过去时 sent 与 lifted off、was 一致。
【判据】上下文都是过去时（lit up, lifted off），此处需用过去式。
【排错】B 项 sends 现在时、C 项 sending 非谓语、D 项 was sending 过去进行时，都不如 A 贴合叙事时态。
【举一反三】考点：时态一致。记叙已发生的事，整段谓语时态要统一。
2主谓一致
It ___ liquid oxygen and methane as its fuel.
【还原】It 是单数主语，描述火箭固有属性用一般现在时 uses。
【判据】主语 It（the rocket）单数，客观事实用一般现在时，谓语加 -s。
【排错】A 项 use 复数形式、C 项 used 过去时（此处非叙事过去）、D 项 using 非谓语。
【举一反三】考点：主谓一致。单数主语 + 客观说明，谓语用第三人称单数现在时。
3时态
Ride-sharing launches ___ normal these days.
【还原】these days 表示正在发生的变化，用现在进行时 are becoming。
【判据】题干 these days（如今）提示渐进过程，需用 be + becoming。
【排错】A 项 become 一般现在、B 项 became 过去、D 项 will become 将来，都不如 C 贴合“正在变得常见”。
【举一反三】考点：时态。these days / now 常配现在进行时，表阶段性变化。
4副词
The ten satellites did ___ belong to one owner.
【还原】原文 'did not belong to one owner'，助动词 did 后接否定副词 not。
【判据】did 已表过去，其后用原形 belong，否定加 not。
【排错】A 项 no 后接名词、B 项 some、C 项 every 都不与 did 搭配构成谓语否定。
【举一反三】考点：否定副词。did not + 动词原形，是过去否定标准结构。
5主谓一致
China's commercial launches ___ repeating quickly.
【还原】launches 是复数主语，谓语用 are 与 repeating 构成现在进行时。
【判据】commercial launches 复数，需用 are。
【排错】B 项 is、C 项 was 单数、D 项 be 原形，都不与复数主语搭配。
【举一反三】考点：主谓一致。看清主语单复数（launch 加 -es 为复数），再选 be 动词。

🏺 A Double Moat from Six Thousand Years Ago

素材来源：中国新闻网 2026-10-08、中国社会科学院考古研究所 2026-10-12、中新网·运城 2026-10-08（真实新闻改写，难度适配初三）
In Xia County, Shanxi Province, archaeologists have shared new findings from the third excavation of the Xiyin site. The results were made public on October 8, after work that began in July 2025. Xiyin is a famous name in the history of Chinese archaeology.
The team found two ditches from the Yangshao period, an inner one and an outer one. The outer ditch is about 2,100 meters around, some 860 meters from north to south and 350 meters from east to west. The land inside it covers about 300,000 square meters. The inner ditch is smaller, about 50 meters long and 4.3 meters deep.
More importantly, they uncovered tools for making painted pottery: red iron ore, grinding stones, grinding plates and paint-mixing palettes. Experts believe this area was a workshop where people lived and produced colored pottery, not just a place to cook and sleep.
Among the finds were painted basins, spinning wheels, pierced stone knives and a stone silkworm pupa. Animal bones show that pigs, cattle, sheep and deer were eaten. Plant remains are mostly millet and broomcorn, with a little rice. The stone silkworm links to a famous half cocoon found here in 1926.
Xiyin matters because of its history. In 1926, Li Ji and Yuan Fuli led China's first excavation run by Chinese scholars themselves. A second dig came in 1994. The third, a century later, shows how Chinese archaeology has grown from digging objects to reading whole ancient societies.

三、语法词汇 5 题 · 10 分

Archaeologists ___ new findings from the third excavation of the Xiyin site.
【还原】叙述已公布的发掘结果，谓语用一般过去时 shared。
【判据】全文叙事过去（were made public, found），此处用过去式一致。

【判据】主语单数，客观描述遗址数据用一般现在时。

🔭 Catching Cosmic Messengers

素材来源：中国新闻网 2026-10-21（成都）（真实新闻改写，难度适配初三）
On October 21, at Chengdu Science and Technology Middle School, an academician gave the first talk of a science week. Cao Zhen, a member of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, spoke to students under the title "Catching Cosmic Messengers on the Roof of the World".
Cao is the chief scientist of LHAASO, a high-altitude cosmic-ray observatory on Haizi Mountain in Daocheng, at 4,410 meters above sea level. There, the station catches signals from deep space. It found the highest-energy photon ever observed, helped solve a seventy-year puzzle about cosmic rays, and gave clues about black holes eating stars.
The event was part of a larger campaign: eighteen particle astrophysicists visited campuses across Chengdu. More than ten experts joined, with talks on cosmic-ray detection, dark matter and high-energy astrophysics. Students did not only listen — they built cloud chambers to see particle tracks, used a sandbox model of the detector, and met a digital person called "Gamma Ox".
Students asked sharp questions. One wanted to know how cosmic rays get accelerated to such high energy by black holes. Cao answered using his own road into science, and left time for replies. At the opening, the experts left the students three lines: curiosity is the most original force in asking the universe; keep the question mark in your heart; it will carry you farther than the stars.
Activities like this bring top scientists to schoolyards. With Chengdu running "Science Lectures for All" and more cities joining, a gap between research and the classroom is slowly closing — and some of today's question-askers may become tomorrow's discoverers.

三、语法词汇 5 题 · 10 分

1时态
Cao Zhen ___ the first talk of a science week.
【还原】叙述 10月21日已发生的讲座，谓语用一般过去时 gave。
【判据】全文叙事过去（spoke, found），此处用过去式一致。
【排错】A 项 gives 现在时、C 项 giving 非谓语、D 项 give 原形。
【举一反三】考点：时态一致。报道已发生活动，谓语统一过去时。
2主谓一致
LHAASO ___ at 4,410 meters above sea level.
【还原】LHAASO 是单数专有名词，描述位置用一般现在时 sits。
【判据】主语单数，客观描述用一般现在时，谓语加 -s。
【排错】B 项 sit 复数、C 项 sat 过去时（此处非叙事）、D 项 sitting 非谓语。
【举一反三】考点：主谓一致。专有名词作单数主语，谓语用第三人称单数。
3时态
Students did not only listen but also ___ cloud chambers.
【还原】叙述活动实况，与 did 并列的动词用原形？不，此处为 and 连接另一谓语，叙事过去用 built。
【判据】not only ... but also 连接两个过去动作，都用过去式。
【排错】A 项 build 原形、B 项 builds 单数现在时、D 项 building 非谓语。
【举一反三】考点：并列谓语时态。not only A but also B 中 A、B 时态一致，都用过去。
4主谓一致
Curiosity ___ the most original force in asking the universe.
【还原】Curiosity 是不可数单数名词，谓语用 is。
【判据】主语单数（curiosity 好奇心），系动词用 is。
【排错】A 项 are、B 项 were 复数、C 项 has 后不接表语 the most original force（应用 is）。
【举一反三】考点：主谓一致。抽象不可数名词作主语，谓语用单数。
5词性
Some of today's question-askers may become tomorrow's ___.
【还原】become 后接名词，指“发现者”，用 discoverers。
【判据】题干要填表示人的名词，discoverer 加 -s 变复数。
【排错】A 项 discover 动词；C 项 discovering 动名词；D 项 discovered 过去分词，都不能作 become 的名词宾语。
【举一反三】考点：词性转换。-er/-or 加在动词后表“做……的人”，此处 discover→discoverer。
