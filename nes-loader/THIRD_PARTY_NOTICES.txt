This application bundles JSNES and extracts its AudioWorklet into a separate
asset. Its speaker initialization uses that asset, a 44100 Hz AudioContext,
and checks that the audio context is still active after loading the worklet.
The build also corrects the 8x16 sprite tile address calculation, following
https://github.com/bfirsh/jsnes/pull/784 (IronCodeStudios), also identified in
https://github.com/bfirsh/jsnes/pull/666 (taiyuuki).
The JSNES source is provided by the pinned npm dependency; see its LICENSE.
The application adds its own Mapper 25 implementation and CPU cycle hook,
based on the VRC4 and VRC IRQ hardware descriptions at nesdev.org.
