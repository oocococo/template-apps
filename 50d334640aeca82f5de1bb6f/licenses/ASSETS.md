# Included assets

- `template-files/vehicles/concept.glb.bin`: Car Concept, model and textures by Eric Chadwick, © 2024 Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source: [Khronos glTF Sample Assets / CarConcept](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept). Based on a CC0 concept car by Unity Fan. The bundled model has been simplified and unused material variants removed. The application recolors the paint, changes glass materials, batches meshes, animates the wheels and hides license/emblem meshes. Khronos marks remain subject to their [mark notice](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/blob/main/LICENSES/LicenseRef-LegalMark-Khronos.txt); no endorsement is claimed.
- `template-files/characters/casual.glb`, `suit.glb`: [Animated Men](https://quaternius.com/packs/animatedmen.html) by Quaternius, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). GLB distribution from [Hisensen/football-match-simulator](https://github.com/Hisensen/football-match-simulator/tree/master/backend/static/players), models `man1.glb` and `man2.glb`. Original animations are retained and blended at runtime.
- `template-files/textures/asphalt.jpg`, `asphalt-normal.jpg`: [Asphalt 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_02), Poly Haven, CC0 1.0.
- `template-files/textures/sky.hdr`: [Kloppenheim 06 (Pure Sky)](https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky), Poly Haven, CC0 1.0.
- Barlow Condensed: Jeremy Tribby, SIL Open Font License; see `Barlow-OFL.txt`.

The bundled third-party assets are distributed under the licenses above. Other procedural meshes and synthesized audio are part of the application source.

- `template-files/vehicles/sedan.glb.bin`, `suv.glb.bin`, `pickup.glb.bin`, `van.glb.bin`: Original unbranded models by Innerscene, [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Sources: [white four-door sedan](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/white-four-door-sedan-1f271353), [sage crossover SUV](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/sage-five-door-crossover-suv-1db314ad), [crew-cab pickup](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/crew-cab-pickup-with-open-bed-4f02e0cc), [cargo panel van](https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/high-roof-cargo-panel-van-c4d5145f). Meshes have been simplified, quantized and gzip-compressed. The application adjusts scale, orientation, paint and glazing, batches body meshes, and separates steering, rolling wheels and brake calipers. The bundled Car Concept uses the same compression.
